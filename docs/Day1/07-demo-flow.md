# LeadOS AI — Demo Flow

## Demo Objective

Demonstrate how LeadOS AI manages the complete journey from a new prospect to a potential client.

The demo should focus on the business workflow rather than showing every technical feature.

---

## DEMO STEP 1 — Dashboard

Show:

* Total Leads
* Qualified Leads
* Follow-ups
* Pipeline
* AI Insights

---

## DEMO STEP 2 — Create Lead

Example:

```text
Business:
Dental Clinic

Industry:
Healthcare

Segment:
Healthcare Specialist

Source:
LinkedIn
```

---

## DEMO STEP 3 — Lead Intelligence

Show:

* Lead Score
* Digital Gaps
* Pain Points
* Opportunity
* Recommended Services

---

## DEMO STEP 4 — AI Outreach

Click:

**Generate Outreach**

Generate:

* LinkedIn DM
* Cold Email
* Follow-up
* Call Opening

---

## DEMO STEP 5 — Sales Pipeline

Move the lead through:

```text
NEW
 ↓
RESEARCHED
 ↓
CONTACTED
 ↓

 ↓
MEETING
```

---

## DEMO STEP 6 — Analytics

Show:

* Lead Source
* Pipeline

* Sales Performance

---

## Demo Message

The product should be presented as:

**LeadOS AI — AI-Powered Lead Acquisition & Sales Operating System**

It is not positioned only as a traditional CRM.

It combines:

**Sales CRM + Lead Intelligence + AI Sales Assistant**
