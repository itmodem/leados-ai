# LeadOS AI — Project Scope

## 1. Project Name

**LeadOS AI**

### Product Positioning

**AI-Powered Lead Acquisition & Sales Operating System**

---

## 2. Project Purpose

LeadOS AI is a sales operating system designed to manage the complete journey of a business lead:

**Lead → Research → Qualification → Outreach → Follow-up → Proposal → Conversion → Client**

The system will combine:

* Sales CRM
* Lead Intelligence
* AI Sales Assistant
* Sales Pipeline
* Outreach Management
* Follow-up Management
* Sales Analytics

---

## 3. Primary Goals

Lea**dOS AI has four primary objecti**ves:

1. **Internal Sales System**
   Use LeadOS AI inside IT Modem Solution.

2. **Professional Portfolio Product**
   Demonstrate MERN, AI, business logic, system architecture, and SaaS development capabilities.

3. **Client Acquisition & Software Demo**
   Use the product itself as a demonstration when approaching potential clients.

4. **Future SaaS Product**
   Build the architecture in a way that can later become a multi-tenant SaaS product.

---

## 4. Product Development Principle

**Build for yourself → Prove with your business → Sell as a solution → Convert into SaaS**

---

## 5. Core Acquisition Channels

LeadOS AI will support the user's existing client acquisition channels:

* Cold Calling
* Cold Email
* LinkedIn Outreach
* Offline Visit

---

## 6. Business Segments

The system will initially support:

### Healthcare Specialist

A specialized workflow for healthcare-related prospects.

### Generalist

A broader workflow for prospects across different industries.

---

## 7. MVP Boundary

The first version should focus on the complete core sales workflow rather than building every possible CRM feature.

Core MVP:

**Lead → Research → Score → Outreach → Follow-up → Pipeline → Analytics**

---

## 8. Technology

AI API integration
